FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how we work, who we work with, and how to get started.

What services does Galore Labs provide?

Galore Labs delivers two integrated practices: technology strategy — AI & automation, cloud strategy, DevOps-led digital transformation, and cybersecurity & compliance — and marketing & growth, covering brand strategy, content marketing, social media management, paid advertising, and go-to-market planning.

Who leads Galore Labs?

Galore Labs is led by Uday T. Kumar, Managing Partner (Technology) — formerly of Yellow.AI, Zeta, and EY, with 20+ years leading DevOps and cloud-based digital transformation — and Ru Chatterji, Partner (Growth & Marketing) — formerly of Peepul Tree and Tanishq.

Where is Galore Labs based, and who do you work with?

Galore Labs is based in Bengaluru (HSR Layout) and works with clients across FinTech, BFSI, Telecom, SaaS, and Lifestyle sectors.

How is Galore Labs different from a traditional consulting firm?

Engagements are led directly by senior partners rather than handed off after the pitch, and AI handles repetitive execution so partners can go deeper with fewer clients instead of spreading thin across many.
